{% comment %} W-5a — Design QC or the Design Head revises the Arka during package review; DW-2a adds Arka review, while the Arka verdict is still pending. Rendered by qc_review.html and (DW-2a, answer 4) head_review.html only when the view's `can_revise_arka` is true, which is permissions.design_reviewer_turn() for `arka_revise`: QC at Arka review while the verdict is pending and at in_qc, the Head also at awaiting_head_qc. The template never tests the status itself, so the form cannot appear where design_arka_revise() would refuse it. The closing line reads the version's verdict: a pending version stays pending after the revision, an approved one keeps its approval. PRE-FILLED WITH THE CURRENT VERSION (ruling 7): a reviewer may change just the link, just the capacity, or both; the view refuses a POST that changes neither. The reason is mandatory and is shown beside the new version and in the activity log. Collapsed by default: revising is the exception during a review, not the expected step, and an always-open form would read as something the reviewer is meant to fill in. {% endcomment %}
