Until now a failed attempt reset everything: the designer resubmitted an Arka nobody disputed, re-uploaded a byte-identical archive, and BOTH gates re-approved the same Arka — four verdicts to fix a BOQ line. The error category tells us what went wrong, so anything the reviewer does not tick here is carried forward into the next attempt with its approvals intact. THE BOXES ARE PRE-TICKED FROM THE CATEGORY AND ARE FULLY OVERRIDABLE. The mapping is models.DEFAULT_REDO_BY_CATEGORY, passed in as `redo_defaults_json` and read by the script at the bottom — it is a starting point, never a rule. Only the reviewer knows whether a layout error is fixable in CAD alone. `redo_scope_submitted` is a hidden marker so the view can tell "this form carried the checkboxes and the reviewer unticked them all" from "this POST predates the feature". Without it an empty selection is ambiguous, and the two cases need opposite answers. ARKA IMPLIES CAD, enforced in the view AND mirrored here so the reviewer sees it happen rather than being refused after submitting: every drawing records the Arka version it was made from, so a CAD carried across a new Arka would be paired to a superseded layout.
