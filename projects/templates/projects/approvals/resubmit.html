WHAT A RESUBMIT CAN CARRY (D-A18, D-A20): the request's revisable details, pre-filled — title, description, vendor, the three scope lists — of which the view sends ONLY the changed ones; design sign-off shown read-only. The material lines table (D-A28), pre-filled from the live lines with each line's id hidden in its row, sent whole: lines may be edited, added ("+ Add line") or removed (Remove), at least one — the same _lines_editor.html as create.html, with its phone layout and script. A request raised before lines shows its old make, specification and quantity read-only under "Recorded before line items", above an empty row. For each party whose step in the round that asked for changes is approved, a "Keep 's approval" box: ticking it reveals the reason box (CSS only, no script — .ap-keep:checked ~ .ap-keep-reason), so a box ticked before a refusal is open again when the page is redrawn. An approver per party, as before. The note, and new attachments. The hidden revise=1 tells the view these fields were drawn: without it nothing is revised. A refusal redraws this page with what was typed; files must be chosen again (a browser never re-fills a file input). BUILT FOR 380 px: every card is one column, every input full-width, the keep box and its reason stacked under the approver select, long text breaks rather than scrolling sideways. Approvals 3a: a pre-dispatch request's vendor is its PO/PI record's, so it is drawn as text with no input — approval_forms.parse_revision also ignores a posted vendor for it. Contractor bills 4b-1: a bill (`bill` set, approval_views._bill_resubmit) is revised here in place of the request, material and scope cards: its bill number, date, amount, tasks, title and description, and optionally a new PDF (the current one is linked, signed). The contractor and site are text — locked. The raise page's rules carry over: every required field is `required`, the task list is required as a group by a small script, the warnings come back with "Resubmit anyway", and after a re-render in which a new PDF was chosen a banner and a red line say to attach it again. The same script hides and unticks the Site Engineer's keep box once the ticked tasks differ from the bill's (B); the server refuses it too, and does not draw the box after such a re-render.
