{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} Approvals S2a — one approval request (approval_views.approval_detail). BUILT FOR A PHONE FIRST. PMs and the Design Head decide from their phones, so nothing here is a table: steps are stacked blocks, action buttons are full-width and stacked (d-grid), textareas are full-width, attachments are tappable list rows, and long text breaks (text-break) rather than pushing the page sideways. Every round is drawn, newest first. A closed step is read-only to anyone who may read the request. Each form is drawn only where its predicate passed in the view; its view asks again on POST. A proxy step always says so: "Recorded by from ". Turnaround is the view's step_turnaround(): decided_at − activated_at from the STEP ROW. A kept (carried) step is never timed: it reads "Not timed (kept)". Approvals 2a-2: each round draws the details its approvers saw (_round_details.html, from the round's snapshot, or the current values under a label saying there is none) and, from round 2, what changed since the round before. A kept step says whose approval it keeps, from which round, who kept it and why. A proxy's evidence files sit under its step. History lists every action with who and when, in IST (settings.TIME_ZONE). Approvals 3a: a pre-dispatch request shows "Dispatch against" — its PO/PI record (PO and PI numbers, vendor, recorded date) and any linked pre-order approval, from approval_views._dispatch_against(). The record-page link and the pre-order link are drawn only where the viewer may open them; the numbers and title are shown to every reader. Approvals 3b: an approved pre-order request shows "PO/PI records covered" (_order_links.html) — its linked records, SCM's link form and remove action, and the removed links struck through. History lists each link and each removal. Material lines (D-A28): the Request card and each round draw the material as a lines table (#, the material with its make and specification, the quantity with its unit) — three columns, the one table on this page, narrow enough for a phone. A request raised before lines draws its old make, specification and quantity under "Recorded before line items", only when one is non-empty. The change list names each line that changed and each field of it, before and after (approval_views.round_changes). Contractor bills 4a-2: a bill's Request card draws its bill (_bill_block.html, from approval_views._bill_now) in place of the vendor, sign-off and scope rows — the site is in the bill, and a bill takes no sign-off. Each round draws the bill its snapshot recorded. The PDF link is signed per render and expires; it is never an attachment row's link. Contractor bills 4a-3 (D-A53): a viewer who is only the bill's Site Engineer gets the work-only bill block (no amount, number, date or PDF) and the Site Engineer's note. The Site Engineer step reads "Work confirmed" / "Work not done", lists its site photos, and is answered with two forms: "Confirm work done" (photos required) and "Work not done" (a note required, photos optional). A kept Site Engineer step lists "Photos from round N". SCM's proxy form on that step offers the same two outcomes. Payments 5b: an approved bill shows its payments (_bill_payments.html) under the Request card — for every full reader, never the bill's Site Engineer — with the approver's and SCM's actions and SCM's "Request payment from Finance". {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Approval #{{ approval.pk }} — SolarPMS{% endblock %} {% block extra_head %} {% endblock %} {% block content %}
