{% comment %} Payments 5b — an approved contractor bill's payments (approval_views._bill_payments), drawn from `bp`. Who sees it is the VIEW's decision: every reader who sees the bill in full (sees_bill) — SCM, CEO, Admin, System Admin, Finance on a forwarded bill, and the PM on the bill, read-only. The bill's Site Engineer is never given `bp`, so none of this is drawn for them. The money line is the bill's: billed, committed (every payment not rejected), paid, and what is left to request. Each payment is a stacked block, not a table row — this page is built for a phone first. Its actions are the order page's own (order_views._payment_row flags, the same views and forms): Approve / Hold / Reject for an approver, Respond for SCM on an open hold. Each sits behind
, like every other action on this page; each view asks its predicate again on POST and returns here (#payments). "Request payment from Finance" is SCM's, on a separate keyed page (approval_bill_payment), drawn only while something is left to request. A deleted site does not stop payment (ruling Q3); the section says so. {% endcomment %}
