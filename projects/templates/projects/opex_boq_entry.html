WHY THIS SCREEN EXISTS RATHER THAN A MODE OF boq_detail. The OPEX catalogue is 207 items across 16 categories; a site uses a few dozen. Pre-populating all 207 per site, the way the 37-item Residential template is pre-populated, would mean scrolling past roughly 160 irrelevant rows on every site in a tender. The sheet starts empty and the designer adds what the site uses. Residential BOQ entry is untouched — it is still boq_detail. THE JS HOLDS A DRAFT, THE FORM IS THE TRUTH. Adding and removing rows is client-side so it is instant, and the sheet is written to hidden inputs inside one form. Nothing is saved until Save draft or Mark BOQ complete posts that form, which is why the unload guard below exists. Every control here is decoration over a server-side rule: opex_boq_entry() re-checks authority and both locks on the POST regardless of what this template chose to render. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}BOQ — {{ project.project_id }} — SolarPMS{% endblock %} {% block extra_head %} {% endblock %} {% block content %}
