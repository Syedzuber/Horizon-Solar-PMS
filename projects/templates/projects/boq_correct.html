WHAT IS NOT ON THIS SCREEN, AND WHY IT IS SAID HERE RATHER THAN LEFT TO BE NOTICED: there is NO REMOVE CONTROL on any row, on-catalogue or off. Removing a line is not a reviewer capability — it is deferred whole, not half-built — and the server agrees: the only two actions boq_correct() answers are `add_line` and `set_quantity`, and any other action on that endpoint is refused with a message. A hidden button is not a permission and an absent one is not either; the refusal is server-side and this template is the view of it, not the enforcement of it. ONE FORM PER ROW, NOT ONE FORM FOR THE SHEET. The picker posts the whole sheet, which is what lets absence mean "delete". Here every form names exactly the row it acts on, so a partial, stale or truncated POST can only ever add or change — never remove. The quantity boxes are pre-filled with the CURRENT value, so a reviewer who submits one without editing it is told nothing changed rather than having a no-op recorded against their name. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Correct BOQ — {{ project.project_id }} — SolarPMS{% endblock %} {% block content %}
