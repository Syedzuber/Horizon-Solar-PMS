Rendered at the top of task_detail inside #punchPointsBlock and, out of band, by _task_approval_response.html after an approve or reject, so the card is never stale after either. A defect register, not the Issues register: these rows come from PunchPoint. The Issues panel reads `task_issues` and is untouched, so nothing here can change its contents or its count. The card is hidden when there are none, so a task that has never had a punch point looks exactly as it did before. TWO SOURCES (PP-1, PP-5), named on every row: - raised by a REJECTION: closes when the task is resubmitted and approved; - raised by a QA/QC VISIT (pp.source is the visit answer): the assignee, PM or a coordinator marks it Rectified with a remark and a photo, and the site's QA/QC engineer re-verifies it - accept (photo) closes it, reject (reason) reopens it. Every round is listed under the point. Either source can be waived by the PM or a coordinator while Open or Rectified. EVERY ACTION IS DECIDED BY THE VIEW (views._punch_point_context): offer_rectify, offer_reverify, offer_waive and reverify_own_round come from the same predicates and refusals the endpoints apply. The template only draws what it is told. FOUR EXPLICIT STATUS BRANCHES, NO CATCH-ALL. An unknown status renders as its own stored value, never as one of the four. Each row carries id="punch-point-": the approval panel, the dashboards, the visit page and the notices link straight to it.
